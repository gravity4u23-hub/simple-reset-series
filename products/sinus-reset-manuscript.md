# The Simple Sinus Reset
## Clear the Face. Open the Exit. Stop Feeding the Backup.

### Introduction
You are here because something is sitting in your face. Pressure, thickness, the feeling you cannot get a full breath through your nose.

This book gives you the free steps that move that material today and the short bridges that keep it from coming right back. The core takes minutes. Everything else is optional and only there because it makes the core last longer.

### Chapter 1: Flare-Day Protocol
Do this twice today when you are really stuffed.

1. Steam 8–10 minutes.
2. Basic saline rinse (warm, previously boiled and cooled water + ½ tsp non-iodized salt per cup).
3. Warm cloth over cheeks and brow for 5 minutes.
4. Neck and collarbone sweep (light downward strokes, ten times).

Most people feel the pressure drop after the first round.

### Chapter 2: Daily Clear Routine
Five to eight minutes, once a day, even when you feel fine.

Steam or hot shower, saline rinse, neck and collarbone sweep.

### Chapter 3: Dietary Triggers Pause
Seven to ten days off dairy, sugar, and wheat. Most people notice less thickness by day 4–5.

### Chapter 4: Next Level Options
Black seed oil in the rinse. Castor oil pack over the liver. Basic vitamin D, C, and zinc.
