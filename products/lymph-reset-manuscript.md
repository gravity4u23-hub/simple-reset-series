# The Simple Lymph Reset

### Introduction
The face reports backup when the exit is slow. Material from the sinuses has to move down through the neck and past the collarbone before it can leave. This book gives you the free 5-minute daily sequence that opens that exit in the right order.

### The Daily Sequence
1. Collarbone area first. Light circles and taps above and below, both sides.
2. Top of the neck behind the jaw angle.
3. Downward strokes from under the jaw to the collarbone.
4. Short walk or a few belly breaths after.

Order matters. Open the exit before you move material toward it.

### What Changes
Easier nose breathing. Less morning face puffiness. The sinus rinse clears more completely.
