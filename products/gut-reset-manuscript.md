# The Simple Gut Reset
## Calm the Source of Extra Mucus and Inflammation

### Introduction
Extra thickness in the face often starts further upstream. This book gives you the free 7–10 day pause and the simple reintroduction test that calms the source.

### A quick note
This is general information from my own experience, not medical advice. If you have a health condition, take medication, or are pregnant, talk to a clinician before you start anything here. Stop if anything hurts or feels wrong.

### Core Protocol
1. Pause dairy, sugar, and wheat for 7–10 days.
2. Simple cooked foods and plain water.
3. Short walk after meals.
4. If residual thickness remains, test one high-lectin food at a time and watch the face. High-lectin foods here mean everyday ones like wheat, beans, lentils, tomatoes, and peppers. One food, a small amount, one day — and stop if it flares you up badly. This is my approach from experience, not a medical test.
