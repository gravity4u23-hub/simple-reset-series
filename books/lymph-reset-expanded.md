# The Simple Lymph Reset — Expanded Core

Open the routes everything else needs to drain.

## A quick note
This is general information from my own experience, not medical advice. If you have a health condition, take medication, or are pregnant, talk to a clinician before you start anything here. Stop if anything hurts or feels wrong.

## Why this first
The face reports backup when the exit is slow. One-third of the body’s lymph nodes sit from the neck up. Material from the sinuses has to move down through the neck and past the collarbone area before it can leave. If that route is congested, rinsing the face only pushes fluid into a traffic jam.

## Daily 5-minute sequence
Do this once a day.
1. Collarbone area first. Light circles and taps above and below the collarbones, both sides. Ten each. This is the main exit.
2. Top of the neck. Light circles behind the angle of the jaw, both sides.
3. Downward strokes. Fingers under the jaw, stroke toward the collarbone. Ten times.
4. Optional: short walk or a few slow belly breaths after. Movement and breathing help the fluid you just mobilized keep moving.

Order matters. Open the exit before you move material toward it.

## What you should notice
Easier breathing through the nose within a few days if you also do the sinus daily clear. Less morning puffiness in the face. The flare-day rinse clears more completely.
