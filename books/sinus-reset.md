# The Simple Sinus Reset
Clear the face. Open the exit. Stop feeding the backup.

## Intro
You are here because something is sitting in your face. Pressure, thickness, the feeling you cannot get a full breath through your nose. This book gives you the free steps that move that material today and the short bridges that keep it from coming right back. The core takes minutes. Everything else is optional and only there because it makes the core last longer.

## Flare-Day Protocol
Do this twice today. About 15–20 minutes the first round.
1. Steam 8–10 minutes (hot shower or bowl + towel). Breathe slow.
2. Basic saline rinse right after. Warm, previously boiled and cooled (or distilled) water + roughly ½ tsp non-iodized salt per cup. Lean forward, let it flow, clear gently. Do not use plain tap water.
3. Warm cloth over cheeks and brow for 5 minutes.
4. Neck and collarbone sweep: light fingers under the jaw, stroke downward to the collarbone, then circle above and below the collarbones. Ten times. Opens the exit.

Most people feel the pressure drop after the first round. Repeat later if still blocked. The face does not empty on its own. Fluid and mucus have to move down through the neck lymph and past the collarbone.

## Daily Clear Routine
Do this once a day, even when you feel fine. About 5–8 minutes.
1. Short steam or hot shower (3–5 minutes).
2. Basic saline rinse.
3. Neck and collarbone sweep (same light strokes, ten times).
4. Optional: warm cloth for 1–2 minutes if anything still feels tight.

## Dietary Triggers Pause
For 7–10 days, pause dairy, sugar, and wheat/refined grains. Free. Works with the daily clear. Most people notice less thickness by day 4–5. Add them back one at a time and watch the face. Wheat covers the biggest everyday lectin source. If the pause helps but some thickness remains, the next map is in The Simple Gut Reset.
