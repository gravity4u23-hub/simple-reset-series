# The Simple Gut Reset
Calm the source of extra mucus and inflammation.

## Core Protocol
1. 7–10 day pause on dairy, sugar, and wheat.
2. Simple cooked foods and plain water.
3. Short walk after meals.
4. If residual thickness remains, test one high-lectin food at a time.
