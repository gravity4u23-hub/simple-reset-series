# Cover Direction

## Style
Simple, high-contrast, readable at thumbnail size. Clean background. Bold short title. Minimal imagery.

## Sinus
Dark or deep teal background. White or light text: “The Simple Sinus Reset”. Small subtitle line. Optional subtle nose/face outline or lymph path line.

## Lymph
Same family. Slightly different accent color (soft green or blue). Title dominant.

## Liver
Warm neutral or deep green. Consistent type treatment.

## Gut
Earthy or clean off-white with strong dark text.

All four must look like one series when viewed together. Title is the hero. No clutter.
