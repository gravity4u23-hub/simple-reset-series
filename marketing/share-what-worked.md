# Share What Worked

Headline: Show what cleared you.

Post a short video or note:
- What you did (the sequence or the pause)
- What changed (pressure dropped, breathing easier, thickness less)
- How many days

Your post goes to the results sheet (form → email → sheet, see automation/ZAPIER-OUTLINE.md). Your story gives someone else permission to start the same steps.

Example:
“Did the flare-day sequence twice. Pressure dropped after the first round. Neck sweep made the rinse actually clear instead of just move. Day 3 of the dairy-sugar-wheat pause and the thickness is way down.”
