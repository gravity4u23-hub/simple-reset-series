# Amazon Bestseller Mastery — The Simple Reset Series

Highest-leverage methods for nonfiction series on KDP (2025–2026 data).

## 1. Series Read-Through Is the Real Money
Book 1 is often only ~30% of total revenue from a reader. Books 2–4 generate the rest.  
Action: Have the next book drafted or on pre-order before launching the first. Point every reader at the next title inside the book, in emails, and in videos.

## 2. Title + Subtitle Formula
- Main title: short, memorable (The Simple Sinus Reset)
- Subtitle: keyword-loaded promise (Clear Face Pressure, Open the Exit, Stop the Backup in Minutes)

## 3. Categories (3 max)
Pick accurate, specific shelves where you can actually rank.  
Suggested starting paths for Sinus:
- Health, Fitness & Dieting > Alternative Medicine > Holistic
- Health, Fitness & Dieting > Diseases & Physical Ailments > Respiratory
- Self-Help > Self-Esteem or Personal Transformation (if framing fits)

Check live Amazon category pages. Prefer shelves where top books have under 100–200 reviews if possible.

## 4. Keywords (7 slots)
Use long-tail, high-intent phrases readers actually type.  
Already drafted in marketing/keywords.md. Fill all 7. Review after 2–4 weeks and swap any that are not ranking.

## 5. Launch Sequence That Creates Momentum
- Price at $0.99 or free for first 5–7 days to seed velocity and reviews
- Drive outside traffic (short videos + email list) on the same days
- Ask for honest reviews from early readers who used the protocols
- Move to $4.99–$9.99 after the initial push

## 6. Conversion Page
- Professional-looking cover (simple, high-contrast, readable thumbnail)
- First lines of description must state the outcome clearly
- Look Inside sample shows the actual protocol steps
- A+ Content later once reviews exist

## 7. Ongoing Engine
- Daily short-form video of one protocol or result
- Email list from the free checklist
- Share-what-worked posts become new content and social proof
- Publish the next book in the series within 2–4 weeks

## What You Can Study / Execute
1. Amazon autocomplete for exact search phrases
2. Top 10 books in chosen categories (reviews, BSR, covers, subtitles)
3. KDP reports after launch (which keywords and traffic sources convert)
4. Series read-through tracking once book 2 is live
