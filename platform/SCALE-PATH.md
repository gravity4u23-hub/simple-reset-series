# Scale Path — From Strong Foundation to High Revenue

The books + organic engine get you to solid five figures. This is the next layer.

## Phase 1: Own the Audience (0–90 days)
- Landing page + email sequence already drafted
- Daily short videos driving to the free checklist and book 1
- Share-what-worked collecting real results
- Goal: 5k–10k email subscribers who have used at least one protocol

## Phase 2: Series Completion + Read-Through (30–90 days)
- All four books live and linked
- Email and in-book bridges move people book 1 → 2 → 3 → 4
- Track read-through rate
- Goal: 30%+ of book 1 buyers purchase at least one more book

## Phase 3: First Backend Offer (90–180 days)
Options that fit the series naturally:
- Paid community / protocol membership (monthly access to updated stacks, live Q&A, advanced routines)
- Done-with-you 30-day reset program
- Curated affiliate stacks (saline tools, black seed, castor oil) with clear disclosure
- Short video course that expands one book

Start with one. Price it so the books remain the low-friction entry.

## Phase 4: Paid Traffic (once organic converts)
- Amazon Ads on the best-performing keywords and categories
- Meta/TikTok ads using the highest-saving organic videos as creative
- Only scale spend when cost per acquisition < profit from book + email + backend
