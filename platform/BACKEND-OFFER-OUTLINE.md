# First Backend Offer Outline

## Working Name
The Simple Reset Membership

## Promise
The books give you the free protocols. The membership gives you the updated stacks, the order of operations when multiple systems are involved, and a place to post results and get the next adjustment.

## What’s Included (start simple)
- Monthly updated protocol notes (what’s working across the series)
- One live or recorded Q&A per month
- Private share-what-worked space with feedback
- Early access to the next book or major update
- Discount code for any future paid tools or stacks

## Price Test
Start at $19–29/month. Low enough that book buyers can say yes, high enough to filter for people who will actually use it.

## How It Connects
- Mentioned once at the end of each book
- Offered in email 5 and in the share-what-worked follow-up
- Never required. The free protocols stay complete.
