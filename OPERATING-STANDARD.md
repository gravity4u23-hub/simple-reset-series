# Operating Standard

We do not cut corners. We line everything up before we execute.

## Rules
- Every protocol must be clear enough that a tired person can follow it without re-reading.
- Proofread out loud before any upload.
- QR codes and clickable links for every demo video are required in the final books (paperback and ebook).
- Domain, landing page, and email sequence are live before or on the same day as the first book.
- Series bridges, next-book links, and share-what-worked links are inserted and tested.
- Covers, keywords, descriptions, and categories are finalized and consistent across the series.
- No book goes live until the manuscript, links, cover, and launch assets for that title are complete.

## Order of Operations
1. Manuscript solid and proofread
2. Demo videos filmed and pages/QR codes ready
3. Domain + landing page + email live
4. Cover final
5. KDP upload with all metadata
6. Launch content starts the same day

This standard applies to every book and every asset. Cart does not go before the horse.
