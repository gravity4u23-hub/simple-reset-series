# Zapier Automation Outline

## Share-What-Worked to Email/Sheet
Trigger: New form submission (Carrd, Notion, or Google Form)
Actions:
1. Send you an email with the result
2. Add row to a Google Sheet (date, what they did, what changed)
3. Optional: tell them about the next book — no discount code (KDP ebooks have no discount-code mechanism). When a KDP Select free-day window or Countdown Deal runs, point them at that instead.

## New Book Buyer to Email Sequence
Trigger: Manual check of KDP Reports, or a scheduled report pull (KDP sends no per-sale notification emails — sales live in KDP Reports on the dashboard)
Action: Add to Beehiiv/ConvertKit and start the welcome sequence
